## Installing

1. Download and unzip it, then drag **Wallpaper Studio** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS will refuse to open it — that is normal, the app is not from the App Store.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and press **Open Anyway** next to Wallpaper Studio. Confirm.
4. Say **yes** when it asks for permission to change your wallpaper. Without it, the app cannot do its job.

You only do that once. After that the app keeps itself up to date on its own — there is nothing to download again.

Needs **macOS 13 or later** on an **Apple silicon** Mac (M1, M2, M3, M4). Intel Macs are not supported.

## What it does

- **Live wallpapers** — your own videos and GIFs playing behind your icons, with sound if you want it.
- **Dynamic wallpapers** — scenes your Mac draws live, following the real clock: bright at midday, lit windows and stars at night.
- **Explore** — a library of original wallpapers made for this app, downloaded straight into yours.
- **Rotation** — change wallpaper on a schedule, in your own order or shuffled, saved as named sets.
- **Menu-bar button** — switch wallpaper without opening the app.

## Signing in

The app asks for your email once and sends you a sign-in link. Click it and the app opens, signed in; pick a username and that is the last you see of it.
